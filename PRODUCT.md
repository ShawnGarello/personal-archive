# Product context

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Purpose and audience

A precise, curated personal archive for software engineering recruiters, developers, design-minded visitors, and the broad public. Confirmed context lives in [the concept](docs/concept.md) and [experience brief](docs/experience.md).

## Current surface

Wave 1A is a fictional reading study. The desired action is reading the document and following its background link. There is no real portfolio copy or project evidence yet. Final positioning, visual identity, and public contact channels remain open.

## Principles

- Content access remains independent of scene loading.
- Preserve the accepted V3 sequence when subsequent waves introduce motion.
- Keep text selectable, keyboard accessible, and readable on phones and at enlarged sizes.
- Treat the pale portrait page as provisional, with natural scrolling rather than a fixed paper height.

No final brand palette, custom typography, imagery, or production assets are selected in this wave.
